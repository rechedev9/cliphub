# ClipHub

ClipHub turns CS2 demos and stream recordings into edited videos. The terms are the Spanish words the UI shows; code, docs and UI copy use one word per concept.

## Language

### Clips de stream

**Jugada**:
A highlight that ClipHub detected in a stream, with a start, an end and a score. It is a candidate, not yet part of any video.
_Avoid_: Clip, highlight, momento detectado

**Pico de voz**:
A jugada detected from a raised voice instead of a kill.
_Avoid_: Voz alzada as a noun, grito

**Momento**:
A stretch of the stream with a start and an end that the user chose, taken from a jugada or marked by hand.
_Avoid_: Clip, corte, tramo, Short

**Short**:
A vertical video made from one momento or from several momentos joined together.
_Avoid_: Clip, vídeo vertical, momento

**Resumen**:
A horizontal 16:9 video made from jugadas played one after another. It is not built from momentos.
_Avoid_: Recap, highlights, Short horizontal

**Clip**:
Not a term inside the editor. It survives only in the section name "Clips de stream" and as the kind of source to import: a short clip from Twitch, YouTube or Kick, as opposed to a VOD.
