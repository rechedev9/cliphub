# ClipHub

ClipHub turns CS2 demos and stream recordings into edited videos. The terms are the Spanish words the UI shows; code, docs and UI copy use one word per concept.

## Language

### Vídeos

**Short**:
A vertical 9:16 video, made from a demo or from the momentos of a stream. Vertical and Short mean the same: a 16:9 video is never a Short.
_Avoid_: Clip, vídeo vertical, Short horizontal, momento

**Lote**:
The Shorts that one pass of Auto-Shorts makes from the same demo. Auto-Shorts is the function; the lote is what it leaves.
_Avoid_: Auto-Shorts as a name for the group, batch, tanda

**Visor**:
The full-window screen that plays the finished Shorts one after another as a phone shows them, so the user reviews each one and decides to publish it, change it or delete it.
_Avoid_: Feed, vista previa, reproductor

### Clips de stream

**Jugada**:
A highlight that ClipHub detected in a stream, with a start, an end and a score. It is a candidate, not yet part of any video.
_Avoid_: Clip, highlight, momento detectado

**Pico de voz**:
A jugada detected from a raised voice instead of a kill.
_Avoid_: Voz alzada as a noun, grito

**Momento**:
A stretch of the stream with a start and an end that the user chose, taken from a jugada or marked by hand.
_Avoid_: Clip, corte, tramo, Short

**Resumen**:
A horizontal 16:9 video made from jugadas played one after another. It is not built from momentos.
_Avoid_: Recap, highlights, Short horizontal

**Clip**:
Not a term inside the editor. It survives only in the section name "Clips de stream" and as the kind of source to import: a short clip from Twitch, YouTube or Kick, as opposed to a VOD.

### Voz y subtítulos

**Transcripción**:
Everything said in a match's team voice or in a stream, as text.
_Avoid_: Subtítulos as a name for the text, captions

**Frase**:
What one speaker says in one go. It is the unit a person corrects, hides or discards.
_Avoid_: Línea, cue, subtítulo

**Subtítulo**:
One block of text drawn on a video. A frase gives one subtítulo or several.
_Avoid_: Frase as a name for the block on screen, caption

**Corrección del asistente**:
A frase an assistant corrected through the MCP review of a video's subtítulos, after the user agreed (`corrected_by` `assistant`). The vocabulary corrector does not learn from it; a later hand correction makes it the user's.
_Avoid_: Corrección automática, autocorrección
