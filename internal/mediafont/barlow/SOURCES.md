# Barlow Semi Condensed

Medium, SemiBold and Bold are unmodified TTF faces from
[The Barlow Project](https://github.com/jpt/barlow), commit
`dc2940e2e04ef4ec96c07e23e0f02aefbddd343b`, under `fonts/ttf/`.
The SIL Open Font License is reproduced in `OFL.txt`.

These faces provide distinct secondary, player-name and numeric weights for the
broadcast HUD. Existing media typography remains on its original bundled faces.
