common_words.txt is built from 12dicts 6.0.2 (http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/):
the lemmas of frequency bands 1 to 14 of 2+2+3frq.txt with their inflected forms
from 2+2+3lem.txt, lowercased, one word per line.

The 12dicts lists were compiled by Alan Beale, who released them to the public
domain and requests acknowledgment of their use. The 2+2+3 lists depend on the
Automatically Generated Inflection Database (AGID), whose notice follows.
