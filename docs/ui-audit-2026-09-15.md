# UI audit — ClipHub Studio web (2026-09-15)

Code-only review of `web/` against the design system in `app/globals.css`
(type scale `text-meta … text-hero`, `fg-1..4`, `surface-0..5`, `studio-panel`,
`@container/content` breakpoints). No screenshots were taken; every finding is
anchored to a source line. Findings were produced per area and then
spot-checked by hand; one reported P1 (invisible range thumb under
`appearance:none`) was disproved with a Chromium screenshot and demoted.

Global numbers (grep over `app/` + `components/`): 6 `text-white`, 8 `bg-black*`,
5 ad-hoc `text-xs/sm/base`, zero raw Tailwind palette colours otherwise. Token
discipline is strong; the problems below are duplication, states and a11y wiring.

Priority: P1 = user-visible bug or a11y blocker · P2 = clear inconsistency · P3 = polish.

## 1. Systemic patterns (fix these first, they close dozens of line items)

1. **Loading rendered as a negative answer.** `components/shell/capture-readiness.tsx:159-178`
   paints three "No encontrada" badges while `data === null`; `app/(app)/clips/page.tsx:199-207`
   shows the onboarding `HubEmpty` pitch when the local service is merely offline;
   `components/tactical/tactical-demo-picker.tsx:64-85` titles an outage "No hay demos".
   Add a distinct *unknown / unreachable* state with retry in each.
2. **One control, several implementations.**
   - Sliders: `STREAM_SLIDER_CLASS` (40 px), `SLIDER_CLASS` in `produce/music-card.tsx:11` and
     `produce/full-demo-fields.tsx:32` (4 px), inline `accent-stream` in `streams/stream-monitor.tsx:109,138`,
     `streams/moment-range.tsx:63,77`, `videos/library-music-dialog.tsx:160,179`, `clips/edit-options.tsx:100`.
     Only `tactical/tactical-timeline.tsx:105-111` styles the thumb. Promote one `.studio-slider`
     (or `<RangeField>`) with `aria-valuetext`, `useId`, disabled state, and a progress fill.
   - Filter chips ×3 (`clips-hub/clips-lens.tsx:48`, `STUDIO_FILTER_CHIP_CLASS`, `ToggleGroup variant="filter"`).
   - Progress bars ×3 (`studio/long-operation.tsx:75-90`, `ui/progress.tsx`, `.studio-bar`).
   - Status chips ×2 (`ui/badge.tsx` vs `studio/status-tag.tsx`).
   - Empty states ×3 (`StudioEmptyState`, `HubEmpty`, dashed `<p>` in `clips-lens.tsx:141`,
     `streams/clip-editor.tsx:66`, `app/(app)/streams/page.tsx:120`).
   - Panels: hand-rolled `rounded-lg border bg-surface-2` in `clips-hub/match-row.tsx:259`,
     `clips-hub/output-item.tsx:70` instead of `.studio-panel`.
   - Textarea: none in `ui/`, two hand-rolled (`videos/publish-assistant-panel.tsx:263`,
     `videos/review-resolution-dialog.tsx:226`).
3. **Two breakpoint systems.** The shell declares `@container/content` but leaves use viewport
   `sm:/md:/lg:`: `clips/edit-options.tsx:57,203,336` (inside a 320 px aside),
   `videos/publish-assistant-panel.tsx:74` (380 px aside), `studio/empty-state.tsx:29-35`,
   `studio/page-header.tsx:30-32`, `clips-hub/first-run-guide.tsx:30`, `streams/stream-monitor.tsx:78`
   (never collapses; unusable at 390 px).
4. **The long format has four names.** "Vídeo largo" (nav, format bar, upload), "Full POV Chill"
   (`produce/full-pov-producer.tsx:153-154`), "Full Demo" (`:183` CTA "Crear Full Demo"),
   "Full POV" (`clips-hub/clips-lens.tsx:307`). Pick one user-facing name; keep the others internal.
   Same drift: "Clips de stream" / "Tus proyectos" / "Stream clips" / "Mis proyectos";
   "Compra" / "Economía"; three wordings of "servicio local sin conexión"; `REC`/`RENDER` next to
   `PARSEO`/`DESCARGA` in `shell/job-transport.tsx:18-22`; "on/off" in `streams/banner-controls.tsx:110`.
5. **Casing is decided in strings, not CSS.** `loadingText="COMPROBANDO…"` vs "Volver a comprobar"
   (`shell/capture-readiness.tsx:196`), `DESCARGAR` + `ENCOLANDO` vs `Descargando…`
   (`settings/steam-account.tsx:217-254` vs `onboarding/recent-matches.tsx:63-66`), shouted
   `OptionBlock` labels in `clips/edit-options.tsx` vs sentence case in `produce/full-demo-fields.tsx`.
   Keep sentence case in source and let `uppercase` do the styling.
6. **A11y attributes present but on the wrong node.** `aria-label` overriding a visible `<Label>`
   (`clips/edit-options.tsx:313-369`, `streams/clip-editor.tsx:340-357`, `streams/moment-range.tsx:53-80`),
   hard-coded ids (`produce/music-card.tsx:70,86`, `clips/edit-options.tsx:100`,
   `studio/media-player.tsx:405,431`), `role="status"` on static prose
   (`produce/full-pov-producer.tsx:157,173,174`, `produce/full-demo-audio.tsx:25`),
   `aria-live` on a button (`videos/publish-assistant-panel.tsx:291`), live region wrapping a
   ticking progress bar (`studio/long-operation.tsx:52-54`).
7. **Heading scale not applied to headings.** `<h2>` at `text-body-lg` (`produce/full-demo-fields.tsx:9`)
   vs `text-title` (settings); `<h1>` at `text-body` (`streams/stream-steps-rail.tsx:30`);
   unstyled `font-semibold` `<h2>/<h3>` (`streams/stream-editor.tsx:488,642`);
   `<h3>` at `text-meta` vs `text-body` inside videos; h1 → h4 jump in `cheaters/player-detail.tsx:96`.
8. **Dead tailwind-merge workarounds.** `lib/utils.ts:27-29` already extends the merge with the
   `--text-*` scale, so the comment blocks and `[font-size:var(--…)]` hacks in
   `studio/status-tag.tsx:29-39`, `studio/back-link.tsx`, `studio/long-operation.tsx:59-61`,
   `studio/filter-chip.ts:14-20` and `.shell-nav-key` in `globals.css:754-765` can go.
9. **`ui/sidebar.tsx` is still upstream shadcn.** `text-xs`, `duration-200 ease-linear`,
   `focus-visible:ring-2`, a 28 px trigger (`:278`), `SIDEBAR_WIDTH=16rem` overridden to 240 px
   by `app/(app)/layout.tsx:13`. Every consumer pays with override strings.
10. **Data presentation in Análisis.** Percent formatted two ways (`12.3 %` vs `12%`), score three
    ways (`13-7`, split spans, `13–7`), numeric cells `tabular-nums` but left-aligned
    (`players/match-history.tsx:17`, `tactical/tactical-tendencies.tsx:204,262,311`,
    `cheaters/player-detail.tsx:150`), raw `de_mirage` without `prettyMapName`
    (`app/(app)/cheaters/page.tsx:54,174`, `tactical/tactical-demo-picker.tsx:98`).
